Privacy Policy

How AudioNovel Studio handles information when you create content and authorize publishing to YouTube.

Effective date: October 20, 2026

1. Overview

AudioNovel Studio is a creative tool for preparing audio-storytelling videos and publishing content with user authorization. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be processed for these features and the choices available to users.

AudioNovel Studio uses YouTube API Services for its YouTube publishing integration. YouTube and Google also process information under their own policies; please read the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

2. Information accessed or processed

YouTube authorization and upload data

When you choose to connect a Google account and use the YouTube upload feature, the application requests permission through Google's OAuth authorization process. The intended scope for uploading videos is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload.

  • Authorization information: OAuth access tokens and, when issued, refresh tokens or connection state needed to carry out authorized uploads.
  • Content you choose to upload: video files and the title, description, tags, category, visibility settings, or other upload details you provide.
  • Upload response data: information returned by YouTube about a requested upload, such as a video identifier, status, or errors.

The youtube.upload permission is requested to upload content on your behalf, not to read your email, contacts, or private documents. If additional Google permissions are introduced, this policy should be updated before those permissions are requested.

Other content and technical information

Depending on the separate creation features you use, the application may process stories, scripts, narration, captions, images, generated videos, account information you provide, and limited technical diagnostics required to operate or troubleshoot the service.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, including preparing video content, maintaining an authorized account connection when applicable, uploading the selected content with your instructions, reporting upload status, resolving errors, and protecting the service from abuse.

AudioNovel Studio should only perform a YouTube upload or change a video's visibility in response to your explicit instruction or a publishing action you have authorized. We do not sell Google user data or use it for targeted advertising.

4. Information sharing

Video content and associated metadata are transmitted to YouTube when you authorize an upload. Google processes that information as described in its own terms and privacy policy.

Other service providers may process information needed to operate content-creation, hosting, or infrastructure functions where those features are used. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law. We do not grant unrelated third parties access to your Google account through your YouTube authorization.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Authorization information may be retained while a YouTube account connection is active or while a user-authorized upload requires it. Upload metadata, technical logs, and user-created content may be kept as needed to provide the requested features, resolve operational problems, or meet legal obligations.

We take measures appropriate to protect information against unauthorized access, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Information that is no longer necessary should be deleted or anonymized, subject to applicable retention obligations.

For details about the retention or deletion of your specific account data, or to request deletion, contact nguyenming6464@gmail.com.

6. Your choices: revoke access and request deletion

  • Grant access: You choose whether to authorize YouTube uploading through Google's consent screen.
  • Revoke access: You may remove AudioNovel Studio's access through Google Account third-party connections. This prevents further API access using the revoked permission.
  • Request data deletion: Email nguyenming6464@gmail.com with the subject “AudioNovel Studio Data Deletion Request.” Describe the connection or data you want deleted. Do not email your password or access tokens.
  • Manage uploaded videos: Uploads already published on YouTube remain subject to your channel settings and YouTube's controls; revoking app access does not automatically remove uploaded videos.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be able to ask to access, correct, delete, or obtain information about personal data processed by the application. Contact us to submit a request.

8. Children's privacy

AudioNovel Studio is not intended to enable children who are not eligible to use the relevant services to connect or publish through YouTube. Users must satisfy the applicable age and account requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the application's features or data practices. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date when applicable.

10. Contact

For questions about privacy, account connections, or deletion requests, contact nguyenming6464@gmail.com.
