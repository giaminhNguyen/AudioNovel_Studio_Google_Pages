Account ownership matters

How YouTube publishing works

The publishing feature relies on Google OAuth authorization. This information site does not request access to your Google account.

STEP 01

Prepare

Create your video, title, description, and other publishing details.

STEP 02

Authorize

Grant the upload permission through Google's consent process.

STEP 03

Choose

Review the destination channel and video visibility settings.

STEP 04

Publish

Send the video to YouTube when the account owner authorizes the upload.

Your account. Your decision.

AudioNovel Studio's YouTube upload integration uses the youtube.upload permission for authorized video uploads. You can review or revoke connected app access through your Google Account settings.

Only upload content you have the right to publish. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service before using the publishing feature.
